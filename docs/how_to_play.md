# How to play

You are the bartender of Café Tuinema, a café on Mars.
Martians sit at the tables, and the ones without a beer are waiting for you.

- Click a full glass on the bar to put it on your tray.
  Your tray holds at most 11 glasses.
- Click a Martian without a beer to give it a glass from your tray.
- Click the green arrow to walk to the other room,
  where a band is playing.
  The bar is only in the first room,
  so fill up your tray before you go.

Every time you switch rooms, some Martians leave,
new ones arrive and others finish their beer.
The counter shows how many beers you have sold.
